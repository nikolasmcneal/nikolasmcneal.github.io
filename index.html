About

I am currently a fourth-year Ph.D. student in the Machine Learning program @ Georgia Tech, where I work with Ratan Murty. I obtained my undergraduate degree in Computer Science from The Ohio State University in 2023, where I worked with the OSU NLP group.

Much of my work has focused on computational models of human vision. Specifically, I am interested in where they match the brain, where they are misaligned, and what we can learn from that misalignment.

Some related things I am working on:

  • the geometry of visual invariances in models and brains.
  • how the statistics of training data shape DNN-brain alignment.
  • AI agents and RSI for designing experiments and improving models (and running some fun fMRI experiments!).

Lately, I have been learning more about AI agents, safety, and interpretability and will be sharing some thoughts along the way as I learn! [blog]
